# The editor package

How `packages/editor` is put together. Part of the [architecture](index.md) pages.

## packages/editor

No UI framework: the editing surface needs total control of its DOM.

- Who may change the document is one rule in the engine: `Document::set_access` (`Access::Edit` or `ViewOnly`) is checked in `apply_batch` (so the editor, the assistant's tools, extensions, the CLI and the MCP server), `undo`, `redo`, `revert_to` and `try_add_media`; `Op::is_view_only` is the one list of ops that still go through (the field update as a file opens; showing a tab is not an op). The editor keeps read-only as a set of reasons (`editor/locks.ts`: `DocxtorEditor.lock` for the window, `Editor.lock` for a pane) and sets the engine's access from it.
- `docxtor-editor.ts` composes the pieces below into `DocxtorEditor` (bytes in/out, events, read-only, theming); `element.ts` wraps it as `<docxtor-editor>`. It owns an `ExtensionHost`, implements the `document` and `ui` APIs extensions get, and sends key events from its toolbar and pages to the host.
- `builtins/` — the editor's own features as extensions, one file each: every toolbar and keyboard command (`editing.ts`), where they sit in the menu bar (`menus.ts`), their icons (`icons.ts`), find and replace, the command palette, the right-click menu, the missing-fonts strip, and Word/Markdown/plain-text import and export.
- `editor/paste-convert.ts` (the clipboard formats, the converters and Docxtor's own), `editor/paste-flow.ts` (pasting, and the Paste options button that pastes again another way), `editor/drag-drop.ts` (dragging text), `editor/format-painter.ts` (copy formatting and the painter), `ui/paste-special.ts`.
- `open-report.ts` words the engine's codes for the person (`describeOpenReport` makes the notice for a converted, repaired or Strict file; `describeOpenError` says why a file can't be opened); `builtins/import-formats.ts` registers the `docxtor.open.*` importers (one engine call each: `Engine.convert`), `builtins/open-notice.ts` and `ui/open-notice.ts` are the strip under the menu bar and the "About this file" dialog. `DocxtorEditor.toDocx` finds the importer by extension and by what the bytes are, `announceOpen` lets a host that opens in steps hand over the notice, and `openNotice` is what the open document was told.
- `editor/print.ts`, `editor/clipboard.ts` — printing the pages as shown, and clipboard helpers (paste, Markdown in and out).
- `editor/charfmt.ts` draws character formatting as CSS: underline styles (CSS has solid, double, dotted, dashed and wavy; the heavy and long variants are drawn as the nearest), raised text with `position: relative` so the line doesn't change, scale as an inline box per word (measured with canvas text metrics, because CSS can't stretch an inline box), fit text as measured letter spacing, outline and effects as `-webkit-text-stroke` and `text-shadow`, gradient fills with `background-clip: text`, OpenType settings as font variants, text borders merged across neighbouring runs, and Word's automatic colour (white on a dark fill). Hidden text carries `hid` and is shown by the `show-hidden` class on the stage (View, Show hidden text; non-printing characters show it too), so printing and the PDF, which copy the stage, follow it. `editor/charcmds.ts` has change case and the style picker's apply rules.
- Views: the paginator (`editor/layout.ts` and the files beside it) can lay the text out as one unbroken sheet (`setContinuous`: every section gets the first section's width and margins, page height is huge while laying out and the sheet is cut to the text afterwards, no page breaks, headers, footers, columns or vertical alignment); `Editor.setContinuous` sizes it from the window (`width: "window"`) and `DocxtorEditor.setView` applies a `ctx.views` mode (layout, read-only, class, `enter`). `editor/zoom.ts` holds the zoom mode (auto, width, page, pages, custom) and the wheel and pinch handling, `editor/zoom-math.ts` the pure numbers (tested in Node). `editor/snapshot.ts` makes an inert picture of one laid-out page (the sheet and the blocks on it, copied to their places), which `editor/page-grid.ts` (several pages side by side) and `ui/navigation.ts` (thumbnails) show. `editor/folds.ts` folds headings (`data-fold` on a block; the paginator skips it and CSS takes it out of the flow) and holds the Outline view's levels, `editor/fold-marks.ts` draws the arrows in the margin, `editor/outline-view.ts` is what promote, demote, move and fold do (ops `set_outline_level` and `move_blocks`, from `Document::outline` in `crates/docxtor-core/src/document/outline.rs`). Paragraphs carry `data-ol` (their outline level).
- `editor/render.ts` maps the view model to DOM. Every paragraph element carries `data-p`; every segment carries `data-o` (its offset).
- `editor/selection.ts` maps DOM selections to model positions and back.
- `editor/editor-input.ts` intercepts `beforeinput` (typing, deletion by grapheme/word/line via `getTargetRanges`, paragraphs, line breaks, history, formatting), composition events (IME), clipboard and drops, and turns them into ops.
- `editor/tabs.ts` lays out tab stops after the lines are broken: each tab is measured (where it starts, how wide the text after it is) and given the width its stop asks for (`tab-size` set to the end position, or an inline box where that cannot work), for left, centre, right, decimal and bar stops, leaders, the default width, hanging indents, absolute position tabs and right-to-left paragraphs. Paragraphs carry their stops in `data-tl`; `layoutTabs` runs in the paginator before anything is measured, on header and footer parts, and on a re-rendered block. `editor/borders.ts` draws every border style (CSS where it has one, a painted background layer over a transparent border band where not) and `editor/shading.ts` draws patterned shading as an SVG tile. `editor/align.ts` maps alignment between what is on screen and the file's words in right-to-left paragraphs.
- `editor/page-art.ts` draws what a sheet has apart from the text: the page background CSS and a section's page borders. `editor/frames.ts` floats frames beside the text, hangs drop caps in the margin and puts frames counted from the page where they belong (including a page number in the margin, in a header). `editor/note-layout.ts` renumbers footnote marks for restart on each page.
- The paginator (`editor/layout.ts` and the `layout-*.ts` files beside it) paginates a single contenteditable flow laid over page "sheets": blocks are measured once, then pushed past page boundaries with margins; long paragraphs get an invisible spacer at a line boundary, long tables a spacer row. Each page takes the size, margins, headers and footers of the section that owns it; sheets of different sizes are centred in a stage as wide as the widest one. A section with columns is one wrapper (`div.cols`) around its blocks: the browser lays the columns out (multi-column with `column-wrap`) and the paginator sizes each row of columns to one page's text area, so the pages of the section are the rows of its wrapper; a section that starts part-way down a page is pulled up to the page top with a spacer at the start of each column of its first row. Columns of unequal width are laid out by the paginator itself (`placeUnequal`: blocks placed absolutely in the wrapper, column by column, never split between two columns), and a section of vertical text is one page-sized wrapper with a vertical writing mode (`placeVertical`). Other section settings are applied here too: even pages swap their side margins when the margins are mirrored (`mirrorMargins`), a section's text is centred, bottom-aligned or spread out on a page by extra space above its blocks found in one pass and applied in the next (`verticalShifts`), footnotes that do not fit carry on to the next page after the notes part's continuation separator (`noteNeeds`), endnotes can close each section instead of the document (`padSectionEnds`), and chapter page numbers come from the headings found on each page (`findChapters`).
- `chart/` — the SVG chart renderer (`draw.ts`: no chart library; every chart kind including bubble and stock and combinations, axes with nice or fixed or logarithmic ticks, tick marks, gridlines, display units and Excel number formats, the second value axis, legend, labels, trendlines (`trend.ts`: least-squares fits) and error bars; `draw-surface.ts` projects a surface chart onto polygons sorted back to front, or draws the colour bands of a contour map), the dialog's draft (`draft.ts`: the data as typed plus a copy of the model for the look, and the diff that becomes the `options` of one `set_chart`), the colours of the dialog (`colors.ts`), the sample data for Insert, Chart, and the registry that lets a chart be drawn again while it is resized. A chart is an `atom img chart` in the text: the image tool resizes, wraps and aligns it, and `ui/chart-dialog.ts` edits it. `chartex-draw.ts` draws the new chart types (waterfall, histogram, Pareto, box and whisker, funnel, treemap, sunburst) in the same look, from the engine's `chartex` model; its layout maths (squarified treemap, sunburst arcs, bin labels, bar slots) is `chartex-math.ts`, tested in Node. Such a chart is the same kind of atom (`data-chartex` says which), so it is selected, resized, wrapped and deleted like a chart, but Edit chart only says that Word edits it.
- `editor/shape-fill.ts` and `editor/shape-effects.ts` — how a shape looks. `paintFor(fill, defs, w, h, env)` puts what a fill needs into an SVG `<defs>` and returns the paint (linear gradients with every stop, the angle scaled or true, and `rotWithShape`; circle path gradients; rectangle path gradients as a small canvas image; a picture stretched, cropped or tiled with its scale, offset, alignment and flips; the 54 preset patterns as 8 by 8 pixel `<pattern>` tiles), so WordArt can use the same fills. `applyEffects` draws an outer shadow, glow, reflection and extrusion as copies of the shape's path group (`<use>`) behind it, each with an SVG filter or mask, and inner shadow, bevel and soft edges as filters on groups around it. Everything is measured in the SVG's own points (the viewBox is the box in points), so a blur of 4 points stays 4 points when the shape is stretched. The bevel is lit relief (blurred alpha lit by a distant light) and the extrusion is stacked darker copies along the camera's direction: approximations, not Word's renderer. A compound line is the stroke seen through a mask. `editor/shapes.ts` puts the text frame in place: columns, the eight writing modes, anchoring, shrink-to-fit scale, `upright`, and the turn Word gives text in a shape that is flipped upside down.
- `editor/diagram.ts`, `editor/warp.ts`, `editor/warp-draw.ts` — SmartArt and WordArt. A diagram is one SVG over the object's box (viewBox in points, stretched, so resizing scales it): each member shape through `shapeSvg`, its text as HTML in a `foreignObject`; the object is selected, moved, resized and deleted like a picture, and a double-click only says to edit it in Word. `warp.ts` is the geometry without a DOM (a mesh from the box and the preset's adjust values for each of the 40 presets, cut into slices with an affine map each, and the ellipse path for text that follows an arc; tested in Node); `warp-draw.ts` draws the text through it with the text's own fill, outline, shadow, glow and reflection. The plain text of WordArt stays under the bent text (`warped`) and shows while it is edited (`warp-off`).
- `editor/path-edit.ts`, `editor/connector-paths.ts` — outlines without a DOM, tested in Node. `path-edit.ts` turns a preset's SVG path data into commands (arcs as cubic curves) and edits a list of commands: move an anchor with its handles (a closed outline's first and last vertex move as one), move a handle (a smooth point keeps its pair in line), add a point on a segment, delete one, make a point smooth, straight or a corner, and the preview of the smoothing the engine does. `connector-paths.ts` draws the connector presets (`presets.ts` calls it, so a connector is drawn from its preset and adjust values).
- `ui/freeform-tool.ts`, `ui/edit-points.ts`, `ui/connector-tool.ts`, `ui/geo-common.ts`, `builtins/geometry.ts` — shape geometry. The shape picker lists the tools (`ShapeChoice.tool`) and `ShapeTool.onTool` hands the choice to the `docxtor.geometry` extension; the freeform tool and the connector tool take the pointer with a layer over the pages (like the pen) and end in one op; edit points draws the vertices and handles in a fixed SVG over the shape (mapped through the shape's box, turn and mirroring) and sends `set_shape_path`; the connector tool also draws the end handles of a selected connector (the shape tool's frame is suspended for it) and asks the engine for the route (`Engine.connectorRoute`) and the sites (`Engine.shapeSites`), so the editor has no copy of Word's tables.
- `editor/picture.ts` draws a picture with formatting (a `.pic` box that clips the image for the crop and the shape, the outline over it, an SVG filter for brightness, contrast and recolouring, CSS filters for shadow and glow, a mask for soft edges, `-webkit-box-reflect` for the reflection); `editor/object-move.ts` measures an object's box and its offsets from what a position counts from (shared with `float-place.ts`).
- `ui/` — toolbar, selection bubble, image and table tools, header/footer strip, find, page setup, dialogs. Arranging objects: `ui/align.ts` (turns Align and Distribute into `set_object_position` ops: aligning to the margins or the page writes the alignment, aligning to each other or distributing writes offsets worked out from where the objects are on the pages; the arithmetic is in `ui/align-math.ts`), `ui/object-set.ts` (the objects selected together, whatever they are: the shape tool owns a selection of several shapes, and the others a picture or chart joins are kept and outlined here), `ui/selection-pane.ts` (the Selection pane, a dock panel) and `ui/ink-tool.ts` with `ui/ink-math.ts` (the drawing tools: pens, erasers and the lasso). The image tool (`image-tool.ts`) owns the selection frame, resizing, the turn handle and dragging floating pictures; `crop-tool.ts` crops on the picture; `picture-format.ts` and `picture-position.ts` are the Format picture and Position and layout dialogs; `picture-url.ts` fetches a picture from an address.
