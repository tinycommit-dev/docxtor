# AI tool for raw ops

`apply_ops` runs the engine's own ops for anything the other tools do not cover. Part of the [AI pages](index.md).

## Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|

| `apply_ops` | Raw engine ops for anything else, e.g. `{"op": "insert_toc", "title": "Contents"}` for a table of contents or `{"op": "update_toc"}` to refresh one, and `{"op": "replace_font", "from": "Gotham", "to": "Montserrat"}` to swap a font everywhere, styles included, and `{"op": "set_default_format", "format": {...}}` to change the character formatting every paragraph starts from (`w:docDefaults`: font, size, colour and the rest of the `format_text` options), and `{"op": "change_case", "from", "to", "mode"}` over a range. Bookmarks: `insert_bookmark` (`from`, `to`, `name`; a collapsed range is a point), `rename_bookmark` (`from`, `to`; links and `REF` fields follow) and `delete_bookmark` (`name`). Also `insert_horizontal_line` (`at`) and `set_watermark` (`text`, `layout`, `color`, `font`, `opacity`). A picture watermark is `set_picture_watermark` (`media`, `width`, `height`, `scale?`, `washout?`, with the image added through the engine first); `set_watermark` with no text removes either kind. Charts: `insert_chart` (`at`, `kind`, `title?`, `categories`, `series`, `grouping?`, `legend?`, `data_labels?`, `width?`, `height?`, `alt?`) and `set_chart` (`at`, and what changes), with the shapes of the tools above; `set_image` also resizes, wraps and aligns a chart (`width`, `height`, `wrap`, `align`). Pictures: `set_image` (`at`) formats a picture with `crop` (`[left, top, right, bottom]`, fractions of the whole picture, negative pads; the size follows so the scale stays), `rot`, `flip_h`, `flip_v`, `geometry` (`roundRect`, `ellipse`, `triangle`, `diamond`, `hexagon`, `star5`, ...; `corner` for `roundRect`), `border` (`{color, width_pt, dash}` or `"none"`), `opacity`, `brightness` and `contrast` (-1 to 1), `recolor` (`{kind: grayscale | blackWhite | sepia | duotone, color1?, color2?}` or `"none"`), `color_change` (`{from, to?}`), `shadow`, `glow`, `soft_edge` and `reflection`, and `reset: true` for Word's Reset Picture. It also places a floating picture: `wrap` (`inline`, `square`, `tight`, `through`, `topBottom`, `behind`, `front`), `wrap_text`, `x` and `y` in points with `rel_x` and `rel_y`, `align`, `align_y`, `dist` (`[top, bottom, left, right]`), `lock_anchor` and `allow_overlap`. Fixes for the accessibility check: `set_image` (`at`, `alt`) for alt text, `table_props` (`at`, `header: true`) for a header row, `set_doc_props` (`title`, ...) and `set_language` (`lang`, `ids?`). Lists: `set_list_style` (presets `disc`, `diamond`, `arrow`, `star`, `arrowhead`, `box`, `decimal`, `paren`, `outline`, `upper`, `roman`, `zero`, `checklist`, `none`), `restart_numbering`, `continue_numbering` and `set_checked`. Your own lists: `define_list` (`def`: `{name?, levels}`, `ids?`) writes a new list definition the way Word does, and the paragraphs in `ids` join it (the result's `count` is its numId). A level has `format` (`bullet`, `decimal`, `upperRoman`, `cardinalText`, `japaneseCounting` and the other formats Word writes), `text` (the bullet, or the label with the counts in it: `%1.%2.`), and optionally `start`, `align`, `suffix` (`tab`, `space`, `nothing`), `left`, `hanging` and `tab` (twips), `font`, `size`, `bold`, `italic`, `color`, `restart` (0 never, n after level n), `legal`, `style` (a heading to link, e.g. `Heading 1`) and `picture` (a media part added through the engine first, with `picture_size`); up to nine levels, the rest copy the last. `apply_list` (`ids`, `num_id`) puts paragraphs in a list the document has, `set_numbering_value` (`id`, `value`, `continue_previous?`) gives a list item its number, and `sort_paragraphs` (`ids`, `keys?`: up to three of `{type: text|number|date, descending?, field?}`, `case_sensitive?`, `header?`, `separator?`) puts paragraphs in order. Tables: `table_format` (`at`: a paragraph in the table; `width` as `{type: auto | dxa | pct, value}` in twips or fiftieths of a percent, `align`, `indent`, `wrap` (`none` or `around`), `autofit`, `cellMargins` (`[top, right, bottom, left]`), `spacing`, `title` and `description` for alt text, and `borders` with `top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`, `insideH` and `insideV`, each `{val: single | dotted | dashed | double | none | clear, sz?, color?}`), `table_rows` (`from`, `to?`, `all?`, `height`: `{value, rule: auto | atLeast | exact}`, `cantSplit`, `header`), `table_column` (`at`, `col?`, `width`), `table_cells` (`from`, `to`, with `action` `{type: format, width?, valign?, margins?, noWrap?, textDirection?}`, `{type: borders, top?, bottom?, left?, right?, insideH?, insideV?, diagDown?, diagUp?}` or `{type: split, cols?, rows?}`), `split_table` (`at`), `table_cell` (`at`, `action`: `insert` or `delete`, `shift`: `right`, `down`, `left` or `up`), `sort_table` (`from`, `to?`, `keys`, `header?`: the key's `field` is the column number, from 1), `convert_to_table` (`ids`, `separator`: `tabs`, `commas`, `paragraphs` or `other`, `separator_char?`, `columns?`, `autofit?`) and `convert_to_text` (`at`, `separator`, `separator_char?`). `insert_table` also takes `autofit` (`fixed`, `contents` or `window`) and `column_width`. A formula in a cell is an `insert_field` op with `field` `= SUM(ABOVE)`. Notes: `insert_endnote` (`at`, `text`), `convert_notes` (`to`: `footnote` or `endnote`, `ids?`: move notes between the two kinds) and `set_note_format` (`kind`, `format`: `decimal`, `lowerRoman`, `upperRoman`, `lowerLetter`, `upperLetter` or `symbol`, `start?`). References and lists: `insert_caption`, `insert_cross_reference` (targets include `numbered`, a list item), `mark_index_entry`, `mark_all_index_entries` (`text`, `entry?`, `sub?`, `see?`, `ignore_case?`, `whole_word?`), `insert_index` (`options?`), `set_index` (`at?`, `options`), `auto_mark_index` (`entries`), `insert_toc` (`options?`), `set_toc` (`at?`, `options`), `remove_toc` (`at?`), `update_toc` (`pages_only?`), `insert_toc_entry` (`at`, `text`, `level?`, `id?`, `no_page?`), `insert_citation_placeholder` (`at`, `tag?`), `insert_building_block` (`at`, `index?`, `guid?`, `name?`), `insert_table_of_figures` and `update_fields` (`pages?`, `scope?`), with the shapes of the tools above (positions are `{p, o}`). Shapes: `insert_shape` (`at`, `geometry`, `w`, `h`, `fill?`, `line?`: `"none"` or `{color, width_pt, dash, head_arrow, tail_arrow}`, `text?`, `floating?`, `x?`, `y?`), `insert_text_box` (`at`, `w`, `h`, `text`, `floating?`, `x?`, `y?`) and `set_shape` (`at` is the shape's object position; `w`, `h`, `rot`, `fill`, `line`, `effects`, `body`, `geometry`, `x`, `y`, `wrap`, `v_anchor`, `flip_h`, `flip_v`, `name`, `alt`, all optional; see the next sentences for the looks); delete a shape with `delete` over its one position. `set_shape` with `member` (the index of a member of a group or canvas, then of its member) changes that member, with `x`, `y`, `w` and `h` in points inside the group's box. The looks of a shape: `fill` is `"none"`, a hex colour, or an object with a `kind`: `{kind: "solid", color, alpha?}`, `{kind: "gradient", stops: [{pos: 0 to 100, color, alpha?}], angle?, scaled?, rot_with_shape?}` (or `path: "circle" | "rect" | "shape"` and `fill_to` for a path gradient), `{kind: "pattern", pattern, fg, bg}` (any of DrawingML's 54 presets such as `pct50`, `ltDnDiag`, `smCheck`, `zigZag`) and `{kind: "picture", media, tile?: {tx, ty, sx, sy, align, flip}, stretch?, crop?, alpha?}` (`media` is a name `add_media` returned). `line` takes `alpha`, `compound` (`dbl`, `thickThin`, `thinThick`, `tri`), `cap` (`flat`, `round`, `square`), `join` (`miter`, `round`, `bevel`), `miter` and the arrowhead sizes `head_w`, `head_len`, `tail_w`, `tail_len` (`sm`, `med`, `lg`) next to the colour, width, dash and arrows. `effects` is `"none"` or any of `shadow`, `inner_shadow`, `glow`, `soft_edge_pt`, `reflection`, `bevel`, `extrusion` (each an object, in points, or `"none"`; an effect left out stays, and a change to one effect keeps the rest of it). `body` is the text frame: `ins_l`, `ins_t`, `ins_r`, `ins_b` (points), `v_anchor`, `anchor_center`, `auto_fit` (`none`, `shape`, `text`), `wrap_text`, `vert` (`horz`, `vert`, `vert270`, `eaVert`, `wordArtVert`, `mongolianVert`), `columns` and `column_gap_pt`. In `read_document` a shape's look follows its name: `[shape: rect (gradient fill, shadow)]`. A SmartArt graphic reads as `[SmartArt: "Alpha; Beta; Gamma"]` (its texts, which can be read but not edited; it can be moved, resized and deleted like a picture) and WordArt as `[shape: wordart textArchUp "Hello"]` (DrawingML WordArt's text is edited like a text box's). Arranging: `set_object_order` (`at`, `order`: `front`, `back`, `forward` or `backward`) moves a floating shape, text box, chart or picture in its stack (behind the text and in front of it are two stacks), `set_behind_text` (`at`, `behind`) puts it behind the text or in front of it where it is, `group_shapes` (`at`: two or more shape positions, `offsets?`: each shape's top-left in points) joins floating shapes into a group, `ungroup_shape` (`at`, `offset?`) takes one apart, and `insert_ink` (`at`, `strokes`: lists of `[x, y]` points in points from the column and the paragraph, `color?`, `width_pt?`, `alpha?`: 0 to 1, `cap?`: `round`, `square` or `flat`) puts a freehand drawing in front of the text, one smooth path per stroke (a highlighter is `width_pt: 15, alpha: 0.5, cap: "square"`). Any drawing object (shape, text box, picture, chart, group): `set_object_props` (`at`, `name?`, `hidden?`) sets the name Word shows in the Selection pane and hides the object (`hidden="1"`: not drawn or printed, still in the file), `set_object_position` (`at`, `rel_x?`, `align?`, `x?`, `rel_y?`, `align_y?`, `y?`) places a floating object the way Word does (`rel_x` is `margin`, `page`, `column`, `character`, `leftMargin`, `rightMargin`, `insideMargin` or `outsideMargin`, `rel_y` is `margin`, `page`, `paragraph`, `line`, `topMargin`, `bottomMargin`, `insideMargin` or `outsideMargin`; `align` is `left`, `center`, `right`, `inside` or `outside`, `align_y` is `top`, `center`, `bottom`, `inside` or `outside`; an alignment wins over an offset), and `move_object` (`at`, `above?` or `below?`: another object's position) puts it directly above or below another object of the same stack. `list_objects` gives the positions. Outlines and connectors: `insert_freeform` (`at`, `points` or `cmds`: `{t, pts}` with `M`, `L`, `C`, `Z` and `S`, a smooth run through the points, `smooth?`, `closed?`, `fill?`, `line?`, `x?`, `y?`, `label?`) is the op behind the tool of the same name; `set_shape_path` (`at`, `paths`: `[{w?, h?, cmds, fill?, stroke?}]` in each path's own `w` by `h` space, `x?`, `y?`) gives any shape a new outline (a preset becomes a custom one, the box follows the outline's bounds, the text stays); `insert_connector` (`at`, `kind`: `straight`, `elbow` or `curved`, `start?` and `end?`: `{shape: {p, o}, idx}` to glue an end to a shape's site (a rectangle's are 0 top, 1 left, 2 bottom, 3 right, as Word numbers them) or `"free"`, `from?` and `to?`: points from the column and the paragraph, needed when a glued shape is placed from something other than the connector's paragraph, `line?`) draws a connector with the preset, turn and mirroring Word would write, and `set_connector` (`at`, `kind?`, `start?`, `end?`, `from?`, `to?`) changes one. Glued connectors follow their shapes: `set_shape` (move, resize, turn, flip), grouping and deleting a shape route the connector again in the same step. Outside the editor, page numbers are estimated from page breaks. Sources and citations: `update_source` (`tag`, `source`; a new `source.tag` renames it in every citation), `delete_source` (`tag`; refused while it is cited unless `force`), `edit_citation` (`at`, `tags?`, `page?`, `suppress_author?`, `suppress_year?`, `suppress_title?`), `remove_citation` (`at`; in a note style the footnote goes with it) and `update_bibliography` (writes every citation and the list again). |
