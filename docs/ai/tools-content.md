# AI tools for equations, citations, charts and forms

The tools for equations, watermarks, citations, charts, content controls, East Asian text, protection and document properties, then how citations are written. Part of the [AI pages](index.md).

## Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|

| `insert_equation` | A Word equation written in LaTeX, after some text in a paragraph (or at its end). With `display` it gets a centred line of its own and the paragraph is split there. The LaTeX subset: `\frac`, `^`, `_`, `\sqrt[n]{}`, `\sum \prod \int \iint \oint` with limits, `\lim_{}`, `\left( ... \right)`, `matrix`, `pmatrix`, `bmatrix`, `cases` and `aligned`, Greek letters, the usual operators and relations, `\sin \cos \tan \log \ln \exp \max \min`, accents (`\hat \bar \vec \dot \tilde`), `\text{}`, `\mathbf{}`, spacing (`\, \; \quad`). A mistake comes back as a plain message with the character it was found at. |
| `set_equation` | Replace an equation that is already in a paragraph with new LaTeX (`number` says which one, counting from 1); `display` moves it onto a line of its own or back. Word's own equations can be changed this way. |
| `insert_horizontal_line` | A rule across the text width after some text (or after the paragraph). An empty paragraph becomes the line. |
| `set_watermark` | Text such as DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL behind every page of every section (diagonal or horizontal, colour, font, opacity); `"none"` removes it. It goes in the headers, which keep their own text. |
| `list_sources` | The document's sources (Word's Manage Sources) with their tags, the citation style and how often each is cited. |
| `add_source` | Add a source from its fields, or from `bibtex` text pasted from Google Scholar or Zotero (several at once). The tag (`Smi20`) is made for you. |
| `cite` | Insert a citation after some text in a paragraph (`tags`, optional `page`). It is a Word citation field, formatted in the document's style. |
| `insert_bibliography` | Insert the reference list (a Word bibliography field) with the style's heading. It lists the sources that are cited, so cite them first. |
| `set_citation_style` | Switch between `apa`, `mla`, `chicago`, `ieee` and `harvard`, or to a CSL style the app has added to the document (its id; an unknown id is answered with the ones that exist). Every citation and the list are written again. A note style such as Chicago notes turns the citations into footnotes. |
| `insert_chart` | A real Word chart (editable in Word, its numbers in an embedded workbook): `kind` (column, bar, line, pie, doughnut, area, scatter, radar, bubble, stock, surface), `title`, `categories`, `series` (`[{name, values}]`; scatter and bubble also `x_values`, bubble also `sizes`; in a combination chart a series can have its own `kind` (column, bar, line, area, scatter) and `secondary: true` for the second value axis; stock series come in the order of the type: hlc = high, low, close; ohlc = open, high, low, close; vhlc = volume, high, low, close; vohlc = volume, open, high, low, close), `grouping` (clustered, stacked, percent), `legend`, `data_labels`, `width` and `height` in points, and `options` (below). It goes after (`after`) or before (`before`) a block in a paragraph of its own, or inline in a paragraph after some text (`id` and `after_text`). One undo takes it all back. |
| `set_chart` | Change a chart, found by its paragraph `id` (and `index` when a paragraph holds several): `kind`, `title` (`""` removes it), `categories` and `series` (these replace ALL the data, so send the whole table), `grouping`, `legend` (`none` removes it), `data_labels`, `x_title`, `y_title`, and `options` (below). The data goes into the cells the chart uses in the embedded workbook (other columns, sheets, formulas and tables of a workbook made in Excel stay, and ranges grow or shrink with the data); colours, fonts and the rest of the chart stay. |
| chart `options` | An object in `insert_chart` and `set_chart`, merged into the chart's formatting (`null` removes a setting; keys may be `camelCase` or `snake_case`; a wrong key or value is refused with a message). `legend` `{position: r\|l\|t\|b\|tr}`, `legendOverlay`, `titleOverlay`; `xAxis` (the category axis; the X axis of scatter and bubble charts), `yAxis`, `y2Axis` (second value axis), each `{title, hidden, reverse, min, max, majorUnit, minorUnit, logBase, numberFormat (an Excel format code; absent follows the data), majorGrid, minorGrid, majorTick and minorTick (none, in, out, cross), labelPos (nextTo, low, high, none), displayUnits (hundreds, thousands, millions, billions ...)}`; `gapWidth`, `overlap`; `bubbleScale`, `sizeRepresents` (area, width), `showNegativeBubbles`; `stock` (hlc, ohlc, vhlc, vohlc); `surface` (surface, wireframe, contour, wireframe_contour); `varyColors`, `hole`; `look` (1 to 8: the Style gallery) and `palette` (colorful1 to colorful4, mono1 to mono13: the Change colors gallery, written as `colorsN.xml`); `series`: a list of `{index, color, pointColors, fill (none, soft, gradient), lineWidth, lineColor, marker, smooth, noLine, kind, secondary, labels, trendlines, errorBars}`. A `color` is `RRGGBB` or a theme colour, `scheme:accent2` or `scheme:accent2:lumMod=60000:lumOff=40000`. `labels` is `{value, category, series, percent, legendKey, bubbleSize, position (ctr, inEnd, inBase, outEnd, bestFit, t, b, l, r; only the places the chart type allows are written), separator, numberFormat}`. `trendlines` is a list of `{type (linear, exp, log, poly, power, movingAvg), order (poly, 2 to 6), period (movingAvg), forward, backward, intercept, equation, rSquared, name}`. `errorBars` is a list of `{dir (y, or x for scatter and bubble), barType (both, plus, minus), valueType (fixed, percentage, stdDev, stdErr, custom), value, plus, minus (lists, for custom), noCap}`. Everything is written as Word writes it, in schema order, and other parts of the chart are left alone. |
| `list_content_controls` | The content controls (check boxes, drop-down lists, date pickers, text and picture controls, repeating sections) and Word's older form fields, each with the paragraph `id` and number `n` to address it by, its kind, title, value, entries and lock. |
| `fill_content_control` | Fill one in: `id` and `n` (or `block`), and `text`, `checked`, `choice` (entry number) or `date` (`2026-10-14`); `form: true` for an older form field. Locked controls and protected documents refuse the edit. |
| `insert_content_control` | Add a control after some text: `kind` (`text`, `richText`, `picture`, `date`, `dropDown`, `comboBox`, `checkbox`, `group`), `alias`, `tag`, `placeholder`, `items`, `date_format`. |
| `set_phonetic_guide` / `remove_phonetic_guide` | Put a phonetic guide (ruby) over some text in a paragraph, or change the one that is there (`guide`, `align`, `size_pt`, `offset_pt`), and take one away. The base text stays. |
| `set_east_asian_text` | The East Asian font of text, two lines in one (with `brackets`), horizontal in vertical and grid snapping, for text matched by its exact words or a whole paragraph. |
| `set_asian_typography` | The Asian typography switches of paragraphs: `kinsoku`, `word_wrap`, `overflow_punct`, `top_line_punct`, `auto_space_de`, `auto_space_dn`, `snap_to_grid` and a `text_direction` for the paragraph. |
| `set_document_grid` | A section's document grid: `type` (`default` removes it, `lines`, `linesAndChars`, `snapToChars`), `line_pitch_pt` and `char_space_pt`. |
| `set_line_breaking_rules` | The document's East Asian line-breaking rules: `strict`, and for a language the characters that may not start (`cannot_start`) or end (`cannot_end`) a line. With no arguments it reports the current rules. |
| `get_document_protection` | What the document restricts (read only, forms, comments, tracked changes), whether it is on, whether formatting is limited, whether it is marked as final, and where the editable ranges are. |
| `set_document_protection` | Restrict editing without a password (`edit`, `formatting`, `enforce`), or mark as final (`final`). The assistant never sets or removes a password; the raw ops `control`, `form_field` and `protection` are in `apply_ops`. |
| `check_accessibility` | Accessibility problems with paragraph ids: images without alt text, skipped heading levels, tables without a header row or with merged cells, vague link text, low colour contrast, typed lists, runs of blank lines, no title, no language. |
| `set_document_properties` | The file's title, subject, author, keywords and description (not text on the page). |
| `count_words` | Paragraphs, words, characters and characters without spaces of the main text; `include_extras` adds footnotes, endnotes and text boxes. |
| `inspect_document` | What the file carries besides the page: personal properties, the names behind comments and tracked changes, comments, tracked changes, hidden text, headers and footers, custom XML, one line each with a count. |
| `remove_hidden_data` | Take chosen categories out (`properties`, `authors`, `comments`, `revisions`, `hiddenText`, `headersFooters`, `customXml`), one undo step. Tracked changes are accepted; only on request. |
| `set_language` | The document's language as a BCP 47 tag (`en-US`), or just the text of some paragraphs (`ids`). |
| `list_styles` | Styles available in the document. |

## Citations and bibliographies

Docxtor writes citations the way Word does, so Word keeps editing them (References > Manage Sources, Insert Citation, Bibliography).

- The sources are a custom XML part (`customXml/item1.xml`, root `b:Sources`) with its properties part and relationships. The chosen style is saved in `SelectedStyle` and `StyleName` with Word's own names, so when Word updates the fields it formats with its own style files.
- A citation is an inline content control (`w:citation`) around a `CITATION` field (`CITATION Smi20 \m Lee21 \p 23 \l 1033`). A bibliography is a block content control (gallery "Bibliographies") around a `BIBLIOGRAPHY` field, with a heading paragraph and one `Bibliography` paragraph per entry.
- Docxtor formats APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago 17 (author-date), IEEE and Harvard (Cite Them Right), with italics, disambiguation letters (2020a), numbering in order of first citation (IEEE) and page locators. Titles are shown as written, so use the case the style wants.
- Sources come in from BibTeX and CSL-JSON (what Zotero, Google Scholar and doi.org give) and go out as BibTeX.

- Besides the five, Docxtor formats CSL 1.0.2 styles with its own processor (`crates/docxtor-core/src/csl/`). A host adds one to a document with `Engine.registerCitationStyle(id, csl)` and chooses it with `set_citation_style`. The style is not saved in the file: `b:Sources` keeps `StyleName="CSL:<id>"` and, as `SelectedStyle`, the Word style closest to it (IEEE for numbered styles, Chicago for note styles, APA for the rest), so Word still shows the stored text and updates in something sensible. In a note style (`class="note"`) each citation is a footnote whose text is a `CITATION` content control; "Ibid." and short forms come from the style's `position` conditions. Switching between a note style and another style moves the citations between the text and footnotes in one undo step; footnotes that hold anything besides a citation stay.

In the engine and WASM: `Engine.sources()`, `citationStyle()`, `citationStyles()`, `registerCitationStyle(id, csl)`, `citationsAreNotes()`, `citations()`, `sourcesBibtex()`, `parseBibtex(text)` and `parseCslJson(text)`, and the ops `add_source`, `update_source`, `delete_source`, `set_citation_style`, `insert_citation`, `edit_citation`, `remove_citation`, `insert_bibliography` and `update_bibliography`. A citation's text in the view model carries `cite: [tags]` on its segments.
